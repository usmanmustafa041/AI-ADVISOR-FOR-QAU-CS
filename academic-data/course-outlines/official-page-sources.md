# Official course-information sources

The department publishes course descriptions within its programme pages rather
than as a complete downloadable course-outline collection. Retrieved snapshots
are stored under `../source-registry/snapshots/` and are registered with their
retrieval hash. Do not treat a page description as a full approved course file
until the department supplies the official outline.

- BS: https://cs.qau.edu.pk/Bs.html
- MPhil: https://cs.qau.edu.pk/mphil.php
- MS Data Science: https://cs.qau.edu.pk/ms-ds.php
- PhD: https://cs.qau.edu.pk/Phd.php
