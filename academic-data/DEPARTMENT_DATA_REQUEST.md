# Department Data Request

Please provide the following official datasets for the QAU CS Academic Advisor
FYP. Editable Excel or CSV is preferred for structured data; official PDF is
acceptable for regulations and signed notices.

1. Complete BSCS prerequisite and co-requisite matrix, including minimum grades,
   waiver conditions, and the applicable scheme/batch.
2. Current class timetable with semester, course code, section, instructor, day,
   start/end time, and room.
3. Current lab timetable with course code, lab group, instructor/TA, day,
   start/end time, and laboratory.
4. Current registration, add/drop, swap, and late-registration schedule,
   including applicable fines.
5. Latest examination date sheet with programme, course, section, examination
   type, date, time, and room.
6. Official course outlines/course files with descriptions, objectives, weekly
   topics, assessment, textbooks, and prerequisites.
7. Departmental thesis/project SOPs, milestones, submission forms, and internal
   deadlines.
8. Confirmation of the scheme applicable to Fall 2025-onward BSCS students and
   whether the 134-credit scheme supersedes the older 130-credit webpage text.
9. Current fee circular or challan template if fees changed after Fall 2025.
10. For personalized advising, the permitted CMS/student fields and the required
    access, consent, retention, and privacy restrictions.

For every document or dataset, please also identify its effective semester/date
and the office or person authorized to approve updates.
